Your invoices never touch our servers.

Ibis turns a folder of supplier invoices into a checked Excel file, right on your Mac or PC. Every total is re-added; anything that doesn't add up is flagged with a plain reason.

How it works

  1. Drop invoices (PDF, scanned or digital) into a folder.
  2. Ibis sends each invoice straight to the AI provider with your own AI key; nothing goes through us.

    Your own AI key is a password-like code from Anthropic, the AI company, so you pay them directly for reading your invoices. What that means and how to get one (about 5 minutes).

  3. Open the Excel file: invoices, line items, and a "needs review" list.

Tested before we ask you to trust it

On a fake demo set of 47 invoices: 609 of 611 fields right, all 289 line items right, all 9 broken invoices flagged, none invented.

Measured with the Ibis command-line tool that Ibis Desktop will be built on. The demo set is generated fake invoices; real invoices vary.

A real run of today's Ibis command-line tool on 10 fake invoices, and the Excel file it made. Ibis Desktop will wrap this in an app.

Why not an online tool?

  • No uploads to an extraction company's servers, and nothing stored by us to delete later. Invoices do leave your computer, but only to be read by the AI provider.
  • Anthropic says data sent through its API is not used to train its models without your permission.
  • Running cost about $0.001 to $0.015 a page, paid straight to the AI provider. No markup from us.
